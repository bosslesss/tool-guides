Common questions

Can I really run a newsletter on the free plan?

Up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 sends a month, yes. That covers the first months of most newsletters, and it includes the landing page builder and one automation.

How is this different from the free tier of other tools?

The practical differences are the landing page builder on the free plan and human support around the clock. Compare the subscriber caps directly, since every provider counts them slightly differently.

What do I give up by staying on the free plan?

One list and one workflow automation, plus the subscriber and send caps. Segmentation and multiple automations are the usual reasons people move to a paid plan.

What does upgrading actually unlock?

Paid plans scale beyond the free plan's subscriber, list, workflow and segmentation limits. Check the current pricing page for where each of those thresholds sits before you commit.
